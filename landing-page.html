5 Tips to Spot Phishing:

1. Check the sender: Is the domain correct? e.g., example-secure-lab.local is fictional and safe.

2. Hover over links: Before clicking, see where it really goes.

3. Urgent language: "Your account will close" is a trick.

4. Never share OTP/Password: Real IT will never ask.

5. Report: If suspicious, report to IT.
