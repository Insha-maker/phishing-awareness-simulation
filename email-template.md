# Fictional Phishing Email Template - For Educational Lab Only

Disclaimer: This is a FAKE template using a fictional domain. No real company is impersonated.

---

From: IT Training Team <training@example-secure-lab.local>
**Subject:** Action Required: Complete Your Annual Security Awareness Training

Body:

Hello Team Member,

This is a reminder from Example Secure Lab IT Department.

Your annual Cybersecurity Awareness Training is pending. Please complete it before Friday to keep your account active.

Training Portal Link: http://example-secure-lab.local/training-portal

Please do not share your password or OTP with anyone. If you did not expect this email, please report it to it-helpdesk@example-secure-lab.local

Thank you,
Example Secure Lab - IT Security Team

---
Why this is safe:
- Uses fictional domain example-secure-lab.local
- Does not threaten to close account
- Does not ask for password
- Includes reporting email

What we teach users to check:
1. Check sender domain - is it really your company?
2. Hover over link before clicking
3. Urgent language = red flag
